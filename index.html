About

Ranjitha Yellur is a versatile vocalist who moves between Bollywood and film music, Western pop and acoustic, Indian folk and Carnatic classical. She has performed 250+ shows across India over more than five years, from luxury car evenings for Lamborghini, Porsche and Mercedes-Benz to festival and concert stages. Behind the range sit 18 years of classical training: she is a senior grade Carnatic vocalist. She has also sung film playback in Kannada and Tulu.

Based in Bengaluru, India · Available pan-India and internationally
